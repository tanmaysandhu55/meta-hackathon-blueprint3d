Pipeline Architecture

Material & practice.

From raw blueprint pixels to an architectural spatial twin. Four deterministic layers convert raster plans into validated geometric contracts without external CAD dependencies.

META SAM 3.1 Segment Anything model identifies semantic mask boundaries for room boundaries, outer perimeter walls, and door openings. Neural Segmentation
OPENCV + SHAPELY Raster-to-vector polygon polygon extraction, Douglas-Peucker simplification, door deduction, and metric scale calibration. Geometric Processing
MUSE SPARK Spatial intelligence model infers functional room labels, multi-story stair links, and egress topological paths. Spatial Reasoning
THREE.JS ENGINE WebGL rendering runtime with parametric wall extrusion, first-person WASD traversal, and solar azimuth simulation. WebGL Runtime
